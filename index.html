§ 02 — Diagnosis The Market Failure

Narrative markets run on poor intelligence.

The pipelines that move stories from culture to capital were built for a finite supply of manuscripts, scripts, and slates. That world is over. What replaced it has no instrumentation.

01 Supply

AI has made narrative supply infinite.

Every prompt is a pitch. Every model output is a draft. The bottleneck shifted overnight from production to selection — and there is no map for what is being made.

Volume outpacing taste
02 Discovery

Creators and buyers operate blind.

Most decisions still rely on referrals, agents-of-agents, festival circuits, and gut feel. The signal travels through a handful of zip codes while the work is being made everywhere else.

Closed loops, missed work
03 Capital

The wrong stories are missed. The right ones are mispriced.

Without comparable benchmarks, narrative IP trades at vibes and precedent. Capital flows toward what looks familiar, while category-defining work moves through the market unrecognized.

No price for resonance

§ 02 — Why This Matters

The world's greatest stories are being lost.

IP is currently opaque, unpriced, and untradeable. But underneath that problem is something more fundamental: the world's greatest stories are at risk of being lost or undervalued because there's no infrastructure to preserve and amplify them.

A storyteller in Lagos has an oral tradition passed down through generations. But it has no written form. No market understanding. No preservation mechanism.

A writer in Hanoi has a story that resonates culturally but is incomprehensible to Western narrative frameworks. So it stays unrecognized.

A folklore keeper in rural India has narratives that are 500 years old but have no way to reach the world.

KASKAL preserves human creativity by making it legible, valuable, and tradeable. Every story submitted becomes indexed, analyzed, and connected to the global market. Your culture's narrative becomes visible.

The Raw IP is Always Human. We use AI responsibly to systematize cultural understanding, encode and transcribe stories in 100+ languages, help translate cultural context to diverse audiences, trace provenance and ensure creators are credited, and prevent cultural appropriation. At KASKAL, AI is always in service of human creativity—never its replacement.
